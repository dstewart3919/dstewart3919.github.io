How to play

  1. 1. Hear it

    Days, names, maps, planets, and stories speak out loud.

  2. 2. Trace it

    Letters, numbers, words, and names. Fill the shape and hit every drop.

  3. 3. Think it

    Eggs for adding and taking away, shapes, sizes, blocks, cups, and pairs.

  4. 4. Play it

    Hop a dinosaur, fly a bee, stack ant rows, grow tomatoes, find a direction, or zap bugs.
