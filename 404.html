How to play

  1. 1. Hear it

    Letters, numbers, the pledge, the galaxy, and the planets speak out loud. Tap a letter to stop the song.

  2. 2. Trace it

    Follow the dotted shape. Honey fills as you go.

  3. 3. Think it

    Eggs for math, holes for shapes, sizes and blocks in order, cups and pairs to remember.

  4. 4. Play it

    Flap the bee, hop the dino, stack ant rows, grow a tomato, find north, or blast bugs for honey.
